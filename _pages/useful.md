### Templates

- [Working Paper Template (Overleaf)](https://www.overleaf.com/read/dcsmqdxcfzwm#933681): Base template for drafts.

### Trade Data

- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/): The world's most comprehensive global trade data platform.
- [USA Trade Online](https://usatrade.census.gov/): U.S. import and export data from the U.S. Census Bureau.
- [CEPII Gravity Database](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=8): Standard gravity dataset (1948-2020).
- [OECD TiVA (Trade in Value-Added)](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/trade-in-value-added.html): Trade statistics in value-added terms. Related paper: [Immigration, Value-Added Exports, and Global Value Chains in Korea](https://www.bok.or.kr/imerEng/bbs/E0002726/view.do?nttId=10085494&searchCnd=1&searchKwd=&depth2=600346&depth=600346&pageUnit=10&pageIndex=1&programType=rsrchrDataEng&menuNo=600346&oldMenuNo=600346).
- [KITA Trade Statistics (Korean only)](https://stat.kita.net/): Korea International Trade Association trade portal.

### Microdata

- [MDIS Korea](https://mdis.kostat.go.kr/eng/pageLink.do?link=mdisDataService): Firm-level data including the Survey of Business Activities. Related paper: [Investing Abroad, Transforming at Home: An Empirical Study of OFDI and Korean Manufacturing’s Servicification](https://www.eaerweb.org/selectArticleInfo.do?article_a_no=JE0001_2024_v28n2_143&ano=JE0001_2024_v28n2_143).
- [U.S. Census APIs](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets.html): Official API index for ACS, CBP, Economic Census, etc. Related paper: [Does Competition Really Saturate? Revisiting Bresnahan and Reiss (1991) with Structural Fixed Costs](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6451483).
- [Chicago Booth Kilts - Research Data](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/kilts/research-data): Includes Nielsen-type data access options; many datasets require subscription/eligibility. Related paper: [Cross-category Portfolio and Bargaining Power: Evidence from the US Men's Razor Market](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6121267).


### Others

- [Google Earth Engine API](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine): Satellite imagery and geospatial datasets. Related paper: [Watching Trade from Space: Nowcasting and Spatial Extrapolation of Port-Level Maritime Trade Using Satellite Imagery](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5984017).
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/): Search-intensity time series. Related paper: [TGIF: A Quasi-Experimental Analysis of Pre-Weekend Productivity](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/kgs1uth7no8fdj8qo1tec/TGIF__A_Quasi_Experimental_Analysis_revised_Sep_2025.pdf?rlkey=ltxf847rjp60ao1c0v55gtmn9&st=csezr573&dl=0).
- [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/): Macroeconomic and financial time series. Related paper: [Machine Learning-Based Estimation of Monthly GDP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14078).
